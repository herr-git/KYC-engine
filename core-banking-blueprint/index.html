    What this is, and is not

    • It is the author's synthesis of common practice, drafted with AI assistance and meant to be argued with. It quotes no source and is not official guidance.
    • The ratings behind the heat map are judgement calls. The verdict is a plain sum of them with your answers, and every line is shown.
    • Products named are names the author knows of, not recommendations or a market survey. Check what each does today.
    • Rules and dates mentioned should be checked against the current official text.
    • It cannot replace an architecture review, a supplier selection with real hard cases, or advice from your regulator and lawyers.
    • Nothing here uses any real firm's data or configuration.

Ask the rulebook

Ask a question about anti-money laundering (AML) rules. The page finds the passages of the official text that bear on it and shows them word for word, with the article number and a link. No AI model writes the answers, so nothing can be invented. If the rulebook here does not cover your question, it says so.

What this is, and is not

  • It is a search over hand-picked passages from three public texts. It does not cover every rule.
  • Quotes are cut from the official text by a program that fails if a quote cannot be found word for word. The plain-language notes are the project author's own writing and are labelled as such.
  • The EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation applies from 10 July 2027. National laws and your regulator's guidance may differ today.
  • This is not legal advice. It cannot judge a specific customer or transaction.

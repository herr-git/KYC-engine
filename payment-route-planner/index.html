Key terms

What this is, and is not

  • Fees, speeds, spreads and limits are illustrative round numbers chosen for a demonstration. They are not quotes and not current. The scheme names and rough behaviour come from public knowledge.
  • The route score is a plain sum of the moves. Every number that moves it is shown, and you can change it in catalog.js.
  • The bank list comes from the open bank registry in the schwifty package (MIT licence) for the countries it covers, plus a curated list of well known banks elsewhere. It is not a directory, it can be out of date, and being listed is not an endorsement.
  • Rules are summarised at headline level and some are changing. Check the current official text. Nothing here is legal advice.
  • Real routing also depends on contracts, partner coverage, daily cut off times, risk appetite and live scheme status. This page cannot know those.
  • No real customer data and no data from any real firm is used.

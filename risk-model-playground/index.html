Risk model playground

Every customer risk score rests on choices someone made: how much each factor counts, and where the cut-offs between low, medium and high sit. Change them here and see who gets flagged.

How much each factor counts

Sliders set relative size. The percentages show each factor's share of the total score.

Every slider is at zero, so all factors count equally.

Where the cut-offs sit

A weighted score below the first cut-off is low, and at or above the second it is high. You can also drag the lines on the chart.

Safety rules

Rules lift a customer to a higher band when the weighted score alone would hide a hard stop. Switch them off to see why they exist.

Where 300 customers land

Each dot is one customer, placed by weighted score. Left of the first line is low, between the lines is medium, and right of the second line is high. A ring means a safety rule lifted the customer above the band their score alone would give.
